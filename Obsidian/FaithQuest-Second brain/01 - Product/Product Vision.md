# Product Vision

FaithQuest helps churches, Sunday schools, youth groups, and outreach programs manage classes while making participation visible and motivating for students.

## Core outcomes

- Teachers can operate a live classroom quickly.
- Administrators can manage churches, staff, classes, and reporting.
- Student attendance, points, quizzes, badges, and rewards remain consistent across devices.
- Each teacher sees only authorized class data.
- The system remains safe when many people use it simultaneously.

## Non-negotiable qualities

- Secure role and class isolation
- Reliable multi-user data
- Simple classroom operation
- Recoverable data and deployments
- Evidence-based releases
